takeAway lets customers pre-order coffee and food for pickup from partner coffee shops.

- Browsing stores and menus needs no account.
- To order, sign in with Apple (or Google / Telegram) from the sign-in sheet that opens when you add an item to the cart. No demo account is needed.
- Live partners are coffee shops in Tiraspol, Moldova (NoName Coffee, Kacheli, Turka). Orders are paid by card on the bank's secure page (ZAO Agroprombank) and are prepared by a real coffee shop, so please stop at the bank's payment page rather than completing a payment.
- Goods are physical and consumed outside the app, so payments do not use In-App Purchase (guideline 3.1.3(e)).
- Account deletion: Profile -> Delete account (Apple-linked accounts are asked to confirm with Apple, and the Apple tokens are revoked).
- Privacy Policy: https://takeaway.md/privacy, Terms: https://takeaway.md/terms, Support: https://takeaway.md/support

New in 1.8.0:
- The product screen offers variants (size) and add-ons (milk, syrups) from one catalog; the price on the button updates as you choose.
- A cancelled order says who cancelled it, why, and what happened to the money.
- After pickup, the order screen asks for a 1-5 star rating with an optional comment.
- Store pins on the map are coloured by status: open, busy, closed.
- A long press on the logo of the sign-in sheet (or Profile -> About) opens a server switch, Production / Test, which the team uses to check builds against the test server; the app starts on Production and a "TEST" ribbon is shown while it is elsewhere.

New in 1.7.0:
- The order screen shows the pickup code in large type instead of a QR code (stores hand orders over by the code).
- An order can be cancelled only until the store accepts it; after that the screen offers "Call the store".
- When the store accepts an order late, the countdown shows the store's new estimate.
- Store cards explain a short break ("Технический перерыв") or an imminent closing and offer ordering ahead.

New in 1.6.0:
- A store that is closed right now still takes pre-orders for a later time (for example tomorrow morning); "Now" is disabled with the reason.
- Notifications inbox (bell icon next to search, visible after sign-in): messages from coffee shops and from takeAway; tapping a push opens the message.
- The order screen says when the store has not confirmed the order yet or is running late, with a "Call the store" button.
- Menu items without a photo are still shown as text rows, as accepted in 1.5.0.
